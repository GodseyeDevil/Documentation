Hexagon

After designing the pookkalam, I wanted to test a few things. I wanted to experiment with different patterns for new designs. I don't know why I went down this path; I just wanted to push the limits of the design. While researching design references, I came across a pattern. My previous design used a circular expanding pattern that wasn't scalable, but the pattern I found was scalable and could be connected using hexagonal patterns. What I wanted to investigate was how to connect the mechanism using a gearing system. I knew I could connect gears using sun, planet, and ring gears, but while scaling the pattern, I discovered that although it was possible to scale it, the diameter of the control circles increased as I moved farther away from the actual center of the mechanism. I needed to control the rotation of all the control circles so that they would maintain the same RPM and stay synchronized for the pattern to work. I managed to achieve this, and it is now working.
